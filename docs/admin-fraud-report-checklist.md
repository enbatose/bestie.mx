# Admin checklist: fraud / abuse reports

Internal Bestie operator playbook. Not a public SLA. Not a promise to recover money or mediate leases.

**Goal:** clean the marketplace (pause bad posts, curb bad accounts). You are not the police and not a payment processor.

## Where reports live

- Admin → **Soporte** (kind **report**)
- Or Posts → filter **Con reporte** → open report thread
- Categories include **estafa**, fake photos, inappropriate content, chat harassment, etc.

## Triage (aim: same day / next day for `estafa`)

1. **Open the report thread** — categories, detail, linked post/chat, who reported.
2. **Classify in ~2 minutes**
   - Scam signals (deposit before visit, urgency, unreal prices, shady photos, “owner” who can’t show the place) → **pause/hide post**; restrict account if repeat offender.
   - Chat harassment → warn/restrict user; listing may stay.
   - Revenge / no evidence → mark reviewed; leave listing.
   - Unclear → one short question to reporter; don’t leave for weeks.
3. **Act on inventory** — use admin report actions (pause, contact reporter, mark reviewed). Do **not** promise to recover payments or join the rental contract.
4. **Short reply** — e.g. “Revisamos y pausamos el anuncio” or “No vimos evidencia suficiente…”. Remind: no pay before visit + contract.
5. **Close** — mark reviewed. Keep the thread as evidence Bestie acted.

## What not to do

- Promise money recovery or “Bestie will fix the deal”
- Leave a clear scam listing live for days
- Public accusations without basis
- Invent a hard public “we respond in 24h” guarantee in Terms

## Related product surfaces

- In-app **Reportar** on listings, photos, chats
- Messaging **safety acknowledgment** before peer listing chats
- Terms: reporting tools ≠ warranty of safety
